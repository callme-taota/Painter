# V25: glass controls over photographic home walls

## Scope and mode
Local Experience/Operate refinement inside the established Painter homepage. The user explicitly asks for liquid-glass right-side controls over game and music scenes, reducing back to the incumbent solid neutral rail as scrolling leaves them. Code-led, precise extension; no new world or composition choice. Preserve controls, targets, radii, theme menu, other pages and content.

## Motion thesis
The existing photographic game/music handoff is the focal scene. The rail borrows its surrounding image through a bounded frosted lens with a thin inner light edge. Its material strength follows actual image coverage at the rail, including the opening/closing game aperture, and stays continuous across the shared game-to-record handoff. Scroll out progressively restores the original paper, line border and small shadow. No movement or size change of the controls themselves.

## Mechanism and budget
Measure the fixed rail against the clipped game window and the record stage; section visibility alone is insufficient. Use passive scroll, one queued frame while near those two surfaces, no idle loop, no remote request or graphics dependency. Keep all behavior readable without enhancement. Backdrop filtering is bounded to the small rail.

## Preferences and verification
Theme tokens still own glyphs, menus and the base rail. Reduced transparency, higher contrast and unsupported backdrop filters preserve exact original opaque chrome; reduced motion removes easing while retaining spatially correct material state. Validate light/dark desktop/mobile, entering/mid/exiting and reversing, continuous game/music handoff, rail menus/focus, noJS/static/reduced motion and other routes. One coordinated screenshot matrix, a single detector pass and independent finish review before binary-only LAN promotion. No database/content/settings change.
